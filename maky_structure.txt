<section id="journey">
<h2>From a 1:00 AM group project to closed deals</h2>
<p class="lead">I started this degree thinking marketing meant big ideas and good-looking decks. The polished result turned out to be about ten percent of the job. The other ninety happens where nobody is watching.</p>
<blockquote class="quote">Learn from the past. Focus on the present. Plan for the future.<small>My guiding rule</small></blockquote>
<div class="chap"><div class="yr">First year<br>2022 to 2023</div><div><h3>The 1:00 AM rewrite</h3><p>My first semester opened with a group project on the daily operations of a local retail business, with teammates I had known for two weeks. By week three we had a deadline, no shared plan, and a business that changed its product line halfway through our data collection. Past 1:00 AM, I could have handed in something half done. I fell back on my rule instead. I split the work into small tasks, took the workflow analysis and the financial appendix, and called a morning sync. Our professor named it one of the most structurally sound projects in the class.</p><p class="tk">What it taught me: good work comes from handling the small details when things get hard.</p></div></div>
<div class="chap"><div class="yr">Second year<br>2023 to 2024</div><div><h3>My first office role</h3><p>From April to July 2024 I worked as an Administrative Assistant with the Makati Internship Program at the College of Continuing, Advanced, and Professional Studies (CCAPS) at UMak. I kept up with classes while handling student portfolios and admin work for the Dean and Directors. I sorted hundreds of portfolios, drafted memos, and kept the details exact.</p><p class="tk">What it taught me: good admin work keeps an organization running, even when nobody notices it.</p></div></div>
<div class="chap"><div class="yr">Third year<br>2024 to 2025</div><div><h3>Thesis year</h3><p>Thesis took over the year. I did the research and data gathering, defended our methods, and worked with my team to pull the capstone together, all alongside heavier marketing coursework. There were many late nights reviewing literature and refining frameworks. When I got tired, I reminded myself that a big thesis gets built one paragraph and one data point at a time.</p><p class="tk">What it taught me: how to stay focused on analysis for a whole year.</p></div></div>
<div class="chap"><div class="yr">Fourth year<br>2025 to 2026</div><div><h3>Real estate, real numbers</h3><p>In my last year I moved from the classroom into live work.</p><ul><li><b>Marketing and Social Media Intern Leader, Alpha Premier Realty (Aug to Dec 2025).</b> I ran content and lead generation for real estate listings. We audited our content and reworked the posting schedule and ad copy, and the team lifted brand visibility by 30% and lead generation by 20%.</li><li><b>Marketing Associate, part-time, Alpha Premier Realty (Jan to Feb 2026).</b> I engaged 60+ prospective clients, worked through tricky negotiations, and closed about 20% as deals across sales and leasing.</li><li><b>Administrative Assistant, DEPW Budget and Planning Division (Apr to Jul 2026).</b> I tracked the status of the 2026 Annual Investment Program (AIP) and handled municipal budgets line by line, where accuracy was required.</li></ul></div></div>
